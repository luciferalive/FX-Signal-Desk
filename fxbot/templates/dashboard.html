Rules for trading news
    How to read this chart
    • Default view is deliberately clean: the current trade, the few zones closest to price, the main highs and lows, and the moving averages. Everything else is a toggle above the chart. Press Reset layers to come back.
    • Pale green and red boxes are the nearest unfilled fair value gaps and order blocks (overlapping ones are merged, at most four). Amber lines are the main liquidity levels: previous day and week highs and lows and the Asia range. Switch on Minor levels for session ranges, opens and swings.
    • A small arrow is a signal. Hover it (or tap it on a phone) to see entry, stop loss, targets, reward-to-risk and the reason. Several nearby signals are grouped into one arrow with a count. Switch on Past signals to see older ones.
    • The dashed EQ line is the middle of the current swing range. Below it is discount (buys), above it is premium (sells).
    • Coloured curves are the 50, 100 and 200 simple moving averages. Shaded vertical bands are the Asia, London and New York sessions.
    • The blue line is the entry, the red area is the risk down to the stop, the green area is the reward up to TP1 and TP2.
    • 1H plan levels shows the swing highs and lows the 1-hour plan uses (violet). Choose which strategies count with the Strategies box under the chart.
    • Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, press Fit to come back to the latest candles. Times are India time.
    • The Analysis chart is a snapshot rebuilt every ~5 minutes with all the overlays. Live price chart is TradingView's own live chart for the same pair and timeframe.
    • A coloured dot on a pair or timeframe means the bot has a signal there (green buy, red sell, yellow if both).
