How to read this chart
  • Green and red boxes are unfilled fair value gaps. Dashed boxes are order blocks. They start where they formed and run right until price closes through them.
  • Amber lines are liquidity: previous day and week highs and lows, and the Asia range. Blue lines are London / New York session highs and lows. Teal lines are the daily, midnight and weekly opens.
  • The dashed EQ line is the middle of the current swing range. Below it is discount (buys), above it is premium (sells).
  • Coloured curves are the 50, 100 and 200 simple moving averages. Shaded vertical bands are the Asia, London and New York sessions.
  • The green and red box to the right of a signal is the trade: entry, stop loss, TP1 and TP2.
  • Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, press Fit to come back to the latest candles. Times are India time.
  • The Analysis chart is a snapshot rebuilt every 15 minutes with all the overlays. Live price chart is TradingView's own live chart for the same pair and timeframe.
  • A coloured dot on a pair or timeframe means the bot has a signal there (green buy, red sell, yellow if both).
